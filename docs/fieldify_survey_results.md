## Key Takeaways

### 1. Trust is the product
7 of 11 people named trust/credibility as what matters most. Verified credentials ranked above ratings/reviews as the primary trust signal — supporting a formalization angle: users don't just want crowd-sourced opinions, they want institutionally validated workers.

### 2. Payments are a two-sided blocker
Professionals fear payment uncertainty; clients want in-app payment. Both sides are describing the same gap. Reliable, tracked payments are table stakes, not a nice-to-have — and the strongest lever against platform bypass.

### 3. Communication is the #1 professional pain point
5 of 7 professionals cited poor client communication as their biggest issue. In-app messaging scored 4.1/5 overall. This is a must-ship feature.

### 4. Photo uploads and messaging are near-universal
Both scored above 4.0. These are not differentiators — they are expected. Not shipping them creates a trust deficit, not just a feature gap.

### 5. The information gap is symmetric
Clients are frustrated by *not knowing who to trust*. Professionals are frustrated by *unclear job details*. Both problems have the same root: low-quality information at the job briefing stage. Photo uploads (4.2/5) confirm users intuitively want richer context to reduce this friction.
